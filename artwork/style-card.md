# Style Card — Siege City Survival (v1.1.0)

## Style source
Approved by user: **"в стиле референса This War of Mine"** (11 bit studios, 2014).
Ground truth: four reference screenshots from the game, saved in `artwork/references/`:
- `Eu6uS4OUFkJA.jpg` — night exterior, ruined industrial silhouettes, cold moonlit sky, faint fires
- `yNaEqcoH16P7.jpg` — cross-section of a ruined tenement at night, warm window light vs cold grey
- `QeXzAkEWs4La.jpg` — war-torn plaza, rubble, muted desaturated palette
- `8cfuBCuxF8Zc.jpg` — interior rubble hall, dust, searchlight

## Art style family
Desaturated hand-drawn 2D, "war-worn realism": painterly flat layers, heavy vignette, grainy
texture, silhouetted characters, ruined architecture, night setting. Emotional weight over heroics.

## Color palette (strict)
- Almost-black navy (night sky, deep shadow): #0C1016
- Cold steel grey (structures, ruins): #3A414C
- Ash grey (fog, debris): #6E7580
- Cold silver-grey highlight (moonlight, dust): #9BA4B0
- Bleached paper white (window light, smoke): #D8DDE5
- Muted warm amber (window light, fires, candle): #8C6A3F (desaturated — NOT vivid)
- Rust ochre (blood rust, fire embers): #7A4A2B
- Faint olive (dead vegetation): #4A5043

Lighting is ALWAYS night/dusk; warmth is rare, small, and desaturated.

## Lighting mood
Long shadows, moonlit rim light, single warm light sources (windows, fires, searchlights) with
heavy falloff; thick fog/haze; heavy vignette toward edges.

## Edge/texture treatment
Painterly flat shapes, visible paper-like grain, rough broken edges, NO crisp cel outlines,
NO glossy gradients. Textures look like charcoal/pencil + muted digital paint.

## Character proportions
Realistic human proportions (7–7.5 heads), gaunt and weary, heavy coats and hoods, faces often in
shadow; small scale relative to environment to emphasize desolation.

## UI language
Dark paper-grey panels (#1A1E26–#252A33), thin worn borders, stencil/typewriter letterforms,
amber-tinted (#C4A265) text highlights, rust accents. Minimal chrome — UI feels like worn paper
and metal.

## Negative constraints
No neon, no vivid saturated colors, no cartoonish exaggeration, no photorealism, no clean/heroic
posture, no bright green grass, no glossy 3D look.

## References
Game: This War of Mine, 11 bit studios (2014). Screenshots stored in `artwork/references/`.
